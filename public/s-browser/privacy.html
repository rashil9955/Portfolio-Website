Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 (updated November 15, 2026)

S Browser is a desktop app made by Rashil Shibakoti. It runs entirely on your computer. There are no S Browser servers, no S Browser accounts, no analytics and no advertising. I never receive your data.

What stays on your computer

  • Clips and chat: recordings of live streams you watch, the chat messages around each clip, captions and titles are saved in a folder on your computer that you choose. You can delete them at any time; S Browser can also delete them for you after an upload or under disk limits you set.
  • Settings and watchlist: stored in the app's folder on your computer.
  • Account connections: sign-in tokens for YouTube and TikTok, and any API keys you add, are stored on your computer encrypted with your operating system's keychain.

Data from YouTube and TikTok

When you connect an account, S Browser asks only for what it needs:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos you approve; to read your channel name and recent uploads, so it can show which channel is connected and confirm that a video arrived; and to fill in the description and tags of videos you uploaded yourself from a clip, only when you left the description empty.
  • Facebook (Meta Graph API): permission to see the Pages you manage and to post videos you approve to your chosen Page as Reels. S Browser keeps that Page's access token on your Mac, encrypted with the macOS Keychain, and only uses it to publish your clips and confirm they were posted.
  • TikTok: permission to read your basic profile (display name and avatar) to show which account is connected, to send videos you approve to your TikTok inbox as drafts, and to read your public videos so it can confirm a clip was posted.

This data is used only inside the app on your computer, for those purposes. It is not sold, not shared, and not sent anywhere else. S Browser uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok's handling of your data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

What is sent to other services

  • Videos you upload go to the platform you choose (YouTube or TikTok).
  • Kick: watching streams works like any web browser, and Kick's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Gemini (optional): if you add a Gemini API key, the chat messages of a clip (and, for English-speaking streamers, its transcript) plus public information about the streamer (Kick bio, social handles, games) are sent to Google to write the clip's title, description and hashtags and to suggest the streamer's audience profile. Under Gemini's free tier, Google may use this data to improve its products.
  • Anthropic (optional): if you add an Anthropic API key, the chat messages and transcript of a clip are sent to Anthropic to write its title. Without a key, titles are made on your computer.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect an account in S Browser's Settings, which deletes its stored token.
  • Revoke access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok in the app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Uninstalling S Browser and deleting its folder removes everything it stored.

Children
