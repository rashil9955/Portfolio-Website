S Browser

A desktop app for macOS that watches Kick live streams, notices when chat suddenly gets excited, and turns those moments into short, vertical clips ready to post.

What it does

  • Shows the live chat rate of Kick streams and detects chat spikes.
  • Saves short clips of those moments on your own computer, with the chat from that moment.
  • Makes a vertical (9:16) version with captions generated on your computer.
  • Lets you review each clip and, if you choose, upload it to your own YouTube or TikTok account. TikTok uploads go to your TikTok inbox as drafts, so you finish and post them in the TikTok app.

How it uses YouTube and TikTok

You connect your accounts yourself, through YouTube's and TikTok's own sign-in pages. S Browser only uploads videos you have approved, reads your basic profile or channel name to show which account is connected, reads your recent uploads to confirm a video arrived, posts spike clips to your Facebook Page as Reels, and (for YouTube) fills in the description and tags of videos you uploaded yourself when you left them empty. It never posts anything you haven't reviewed.

Everything runs on your computer. There are no S Browser servers and no S Browser accounts. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
